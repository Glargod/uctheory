1. Abstract

Universal Coalescence Theory (UC Theory) proposes that motion, time, and local physical behavior emerge from Pleichyma, an omnipresent pre-Big Bang fluid-like medium (~10⁻²⁷ kg/m³, comprising ~99.9% of atomic volume). UC Theory treats gravity and spacetime as emergent effects of ->coflux (acceleration flux) and ->tflux (time flux), modulated by charge, heat, weak magnetic fields, and local compositional shifts within Pleichyma.

A repeatable charged marble experiment (0.197 s lag, ~5.6 m/s² vs 9.8 m/s²) serves as initial empirical anchor. The theory explores consciousness as an active modulator and offers an alternative lens on the cold dark matter cusp-core problem. This is a speculative framework blending symbolic, narrative, and proto-mechanistic elements. It remains open to refinement, critique, and testing.

2. Defining Pleichyma

pleichyma /ˈplaɪ.kɪ.mə/ (noun)
An omnipresent, conserved fluid-like substrate underlying all phenomena — the source of motion, time, and form. Predating the Big Bang, it coalesces structure from the multiverse void.

Core Properties:

  • Pleichyma is locally conserved within any defined volume — its total substance cannot be created or destroyed, only re-proportioned.
  • Every volume has a dynamic compositional ratio (matter : charge : consciousness φ_c : magnetism : light/photonic : thermal : etc.).
  • Different materials and systems naturally favor different dominant components (e.g., living systems can shift toward higher consciousness fraction).

Etymology & Derivatives

From Greek pleion ("more/abundant") + chymos ("juice/fluid").

  • pleichymal (adj.): Pertaining to Pleichyma
  • pleichymology (noun): The study of Pleichyma and its effects

3. Introduction

UC Theory was born from direct experiential insight (Fractal Mindscape) and seeks to provide a unified substrate for phenomena that remain difficult for ΛCDM and the Standard Model. It does not claim to be a completed physical theory but offers a coherent alternative lens grounded in a conserved pleichymal medium.

4. Theoretical Framework

Key Concepts

  • Pleichyma drives ->coflux (acceleration) and ->tflux (time flow) through local density gradients, charge, heat, and compositional shifts.
  • Consciousness (φ_c) can become locally dominant in high-coherence volumes (e.g., the auric core / dreamer node), altering local dynamics.
  • Shifts in compositional ratios are bounded and trigger compensatory flows to maintain conservation.

Regimes of Interpretation (Important)

UC Theory operates in two distinct regimes:

  • Symbolic / Exploratory Regime: Used for mapping to consciousness, mythic structures (Enoch, ARK ECHO, Fractal Mindscape), and broad conceptual synthesis.
  • Mechanistic / Proto-Physical Regime: Used for quantitative claims and experiments. In this regime, all statements must respect conservation and be labeled clearly.

Mixing regimes without clear labeling is avoided in this revision.

6. Experimental Evidence

The charged marble experiment (10⁻⁹ C charge, 0.197 s lag, ~5.6 m/s²) is reproducible by the author but requires independent verification under controlled conditions to rule out conventional electrostatic, thermal, or air-current effects.

7. Solving the Cold Dark Matter Crisis

Pleichyma density variations and ->coflux offer a possible fluid-dynamic explanation for flatter galactic cores observed in Gaia DR3 data. This remains a hypothesis under exploration.

8. Limitations and Failure Modes

Current Limitations:

  • Amplification and re-proportioning mechanisms (butterfly effects, consciousness coupling) need much tighter mathematical definition.
  • Scaling from lab phenomena to cosmic scales remains underconstrained.
  • Many parameters are phenomenological rather than derived.

Failure Modes (what would challenge the theory):

  • If the charged marble effect cannot be independently replicated under shielded conditions.
  • If no clear threshold or resonance condition for consciousness coupling (φ_c) is found.
  • If coflux/tflux cannot recover known orbital mechanics or time dilation with fewer free parameters.
  • If compositional re-proportioning cannot be bounded without losing explanatory power.
