# Component-Level Adversarial Audit of Universal Coalescence Theory

**Date:** 17 August 2026  
**Status:** Methodological companion document  
**Relation to main theory:** Independent. This document does not form part of the core ontological statement of UC Theory. It records the standards by which specific components may be weakened or ruled out.

---

## Guiding Distinction

- **Mere non-support**: The predicted effect is not observed under current conditions. This is consistent with the theory remaining viable if the relevant conditions were not met, the effect is small, or the measurement is insufficient.

- **Material weakening or falsification**: An observation or logical contradiction that directly conflicts with a core claim, or that forces the theory to abandon or radically revise a central component while remaining consistent with the rest of known physics.

This distinction is applied throughout.

---

## 1. Conservation of Pleichyma + Compositional Re-proportioning

**Core claim**  
Local substance is conserved; compositional fractions (including \(\varphi_c\)) can only be re-proportioned, not created or destroyed.

**Mere non-support**  
Failure (so far) to detect any re-proportioning signature in laboratory systems.

**Material weakening / potential falsification**  
- Clear experimental demonstration that a local rise in one compositional fraction occurs without any compensatory change in other fractions *or* in local temporal structure, while total content appears non-conserved.  
- Observation of unbounded growth of \(\varphi_c\) (or any other fraction) that violates conservation and cannot be absorbed into temporal proportioning or compensatory flows.  
- Logical contradiction: if the theory is forced to allow creation or destruction of Pleichymal content to save other claims, the “conserved medium” ontology collapses.

## 2. Directional Temporal Structure \(\mathbf{T} \in \mathcal{T}\) and Latent (“Ghost”) Trajectories

**Core claim**  
Ordinary scalar \(t\) is a projection of a richer directional temporal structure; alternative trajectories can act as latent constraints without constituting separate spatial universes.

**Mere non-support**  
No current detection of statistical fingerprints of latent trajectories on transition rates or effective potentials.

**Material weakening / potential falsification**  
Demonstration that phenomena for which UC Theory makes a specific, quantitative prediction involving latent temporal trajectories are fully accounted for by existing degrees of freedom, with the UC prediction providing no additional explanatory or predictive value.

(This keeps the criterion focused on UC’s own claims rather than requiring standard physics to have already explained everything.)

---

## 3. Coflux as the Dynamical Response (Spatial + Temporal)

**Core claim**  
Local dynamics (including gravity-like effects) arise as coflux trajectories responding to spatial *and* temporal gradients in Pleichyma.

**Mere non-support**  
No laboratory detection of a temporal-gradient contribution to acceleration or effective force beyond standard physics.

**Material weakening / potential falsification**  
- Precision tests (orbital mechanics, free-fall, equivalence principle, gravitational redshift) that recover standard results to high accuracy in regimes where coflux should produce detectable deviations, with no remaining degrees of freedom to absorb the discrepancy.  
- Demonstration that any apparent coflux effect is fully reducible to known forces under controlled conditions.  
- Failure of the charged-marble effect under independent, shielded, temperature-matched, low-pressure replication would directly undermine the primary empirical anchor currently linked to coflux.

---

## 4. Consciousness (\(\varphi_c\)) as Foundational Compositional Component with Dynamical Role

**Core claim**  
\(\varphi_c\) is ontologically primary within Pleichyma, can become locally dominant under coherence, and can modulate coflux / permitted temporal trajectories.

**Mere non-support**  
Current absence of positive laboratory evidence for \(\varphi_c\) effects. Failure of early candidate probes.

**Material weakening / potential falsification**  
Clear experimental evidence against a *specific, theory-specified* coupling mechanism.

Before a null result can count as falsifying, the theory must state:
- which variable is being manipulated,
- what change in \(\varphi_c\) (or coherence) is predicted,
- what observable is expected to shift, and
- how that shift is distinguished from ordinary physiological confounds.

Broad interventions (anesthesia, sleep, pharmacological modulation) that alter many variables simultaneously do not, by themselves, constitute clean tests.

Evidence that elevating or suppressing the *specifically identified* coherence variable produces no corresponding change in the *specifically predicted* observable, while alternative non-conscious mechanisms fully account for residual anomalies, would materially weaken the dynamical claim for \(\varphi_c\).

The three-way distinction already present in the main theory remains operative:  
“no evidence yet” ≠ “evidence against a specific mechanism” ≠ “evidence against ontological fundamentality.”

## 5. Projection \(\Pi(\mathcal{P}) \to (x,y,z,t)\) and Recovery of Known Physics

**Core claim**  
Ordinary spacetime and gravity are projections of the richer Pleichymal state; the theory must recover Newtonian and relativistic limits in appropriate regimes.

**Mere non-support**  
Absence (so far) of an explicit limiting procedure showing recovery.

**Material weakening / potential falsification**  
Demonstration that no continuous limiting procedure exists that recovers known orbital mechanics, light deflection, gravitational redshift, and time dilation from coflux + \(\mathbf{T}\) without introducing more free parameters than the standard theories, or without contradicting precision data.

This remains one of the strongest theoretical obligations the architecture inherits.

## Overall Assessment

Most existing data do not presently constitute direct tests of the theory’s distinctive predictions, because several core mechanisms remain insufficiently specified.

The primary current limitation is not that observations contradict UC Theory; it is that UC Theory is not yet specific enough for many observations to meaningfully discriminate it from alternatives.

---

## Prediction-Before-Falsification Principle

A component cannot be considered strongly falsifiable until the theory specifies:

1. the conditions under which it predicts a measurable effect,
2. the expected direction or magnitude of that effect, and
3. the observations that would distinguish it from established alternatives.

Without this, “we have not observed the effect” can become indefinitely protective. The principle above prevents that slide.

---

## Immediate Vulnerabilities (Priority Order)

1. Independent, controlled status of the charged-marble effect.  
2. Absence of a conservation-consistent dynamics for \(\varphi_c\).  
3. Absence of an explicit limiting recovery of ordinary physics.  
4. Lack of quantitative predictions for latent temporal trajectories that could be tested against existing alternatives.
